Creavate Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Creavate ("we", "our", or "us") is a creator CRM app that helps social media influencers manage brand collaboration deals. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, Instagram handle, and deal information you enter into the app. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Instagram Data

When you connect your Instagram account, we access your basic profile information including your username and profile picture. We use this information only to personalize your experience within the app. We do not store your Instagram password and we do not post on your behalf without explicit permission.

4. Gmail Data

When you connect your Gmail account, we read your emails only to identify brand collaboration opportunities. We do not store your emails on our servers. All email processing happens on your device. We do not share your email content with any third parties.

5. Data Storage

All your deal data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not upload your deal information to any external servers without your explicit consent.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Your data is encrypted on your device and we use secure connections for all network communications.

7. Third Party Services

Creavate integrates with Instagram (Meta) and Gmail (Google) with your permission. These services have their own privacy policies which govern their use of your data.

8. Children's Privacy

Creavate is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of this policy.
